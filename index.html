KassiFleet
Four mobile apps

Order food, cook it, deliver it, watch the fleet.

Each app is phone-sized and built for one job. They share a single order ledger in this browser, so a basket placed in the customer app appears in the vendor queue, then in the driver's nearby list, then in the owner's fleet console. Customers can have orders delivered or collect them at the counter.

Township only: Soweto, Alexandra, Tembisa, Katlehong and Vosloorus.

Customer Filter township vendors by township, fee, time and rating, then order for delivery or collect at the counter. Open customer app Vendor Sign in with the registered email and shop ID, accept tickets, publish dishes and watch the day's takings. Open vendor app Driver See ready pickups sorted by distance to the restaurant, claim one, then follow the route and its GPS trail. Open driver app Owner console A four-digit owner PIN, then the shop registry, the live fleet, driver routes and payouts. Add shops and issue their IDs here. Open owner console
How an order moves
1Customer adds dishes from one restaurant and places the order.
2Vendor sees it under New, accepts it, cooks it and marks it ready.
3Driver finds a delivery in nearby pickups, claims it, confirms pickup and marks it delivered.
4The owner console shows the same order moving through the queue and the payout.
Collection orders
No driver, no delivery fee. The customer gets a collection code and the vendor hands the order over at the counter.
Collection
Shop registry
Every shop carries a shop ID (SHP-1041 and up), the name it registered with and its registered email. The owner console adds shops and issues the ID; the kitchen app signs in with that email plus the ID. Customers filter vendors by shop ID, name or email.
Owner PIN
Four digits, stored as a salted SHA-256 hash in this browser and never kept in the clear. Rotate it in the console under Owner security.
Hashed
Opens in the browser; no server needed. Keep two tabs open to watch updates cross over.
